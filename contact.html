Shipping & delivery

Boxes ship on the 5th of every month and arrive within 3–6 business days across India. You'll get a tracking link by email the moment your box leaves our warehouse.

💳

Billing & payments

Cards, UPI and net banking are all accepted. Subscriptions renew automatically on your billing date — cancel or pause anytime from your account before then.

🔄

Returns & swaps

Not loving an item? Reach out within 14 days of delivery for a credit or swap — no questions asked, no return shipping needed for most items.

👤

Account & subscription

Update your address, payment method, or box selection anytime from your dashboard. Changes apply automatically from your next unshipped order.

🎁

Gifting

Choose any plan at checkout and select "This is a gift" to schedule delivery and add a personal note — the recipient never sees a price.
