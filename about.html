Our story

Curation, with a human touch

Stackly started in a Bengaluru kitchen in 2021 with one question: why do most subscription boxes feel like filler? We set out to build the opposite.

What we believe

Every box should earn its spot on your doorstep

We work with independent roasters, formulators, authors and makers — not just big brands — to find things you can't easily find yourself. Every item is tested by our team before it ships to you.

Today Stackly ships 15 curated categories to thousands of homes across India every month, and we're just getting started.
